# La Boca & San Telmo — Buenos Aires Urban Economics

**Researchers:** Zenah Itani and Sachhyam Manandhar  
**Program:** University of Texas at Dallas study abroad, Summer 2026

## Research focus

A joint research presentation comparing tourism, land use, transport access, historic preservation, and neighborhood rental-market incentives in **La Boca** and **San Telmo**, Buenos Aires.

## Questions explored

- How does tourism concentrated around La Boca's Caminito differ from more dispersed tourism in San Telmo?
- How are Airbnb rental prices and availability associated with proximity to La Bombonera and other visitor destinations?
- What roles might congestion, zoning restrictions, transit corridors, and preservation policies play in neighborhood outcomes?

## Research approach

The research drew on Airbnb and geographic information, maps, and econometric interpretation. The project discusses potential stadium-area disamenities, spatial variation in tourist activity, and the trade-offs between heritage preservation and redevelopment.

**Interpretive limitation:** The findings are presented as empirical associations and economic interpretations, not independently verified causal effects. The original R scripts and underlying research datasets were not among the files available for the portfolio upload.
